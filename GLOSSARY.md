# myWebsite

Personal site and devlog for Tanvi Shashikanth, an Electronics & Computer Engineering student at BITS Goa. The site presents selected projects and publishes a running log of coursework, builds, and learning.

## Language

**Devlog**:
The running public record of coursework, project progress, and reflection published on this site.
_Avoid_: Blog

**Log entry**:
A single dated item published in the Devlog.
_Avoid_: Post, article

**Project**:
A thing Tanvi has built or is building, presented with its stack and links.
_Avoid_: Work, portfolio piece
